# Helper profile checklist

Use this list when preparing a helper's profile for the website. Send one message per helper in
Claude Code with her details, photo and (optional) video, and Claude will add her to the site.

## 1. Required

| Item | Example | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helper ID | KD-1824 | Unique for every helper |
| First name only | Thandar | No surname |
| Age | 30 | Age only, **not** date of birth |
| Nationality | Myanmar | |
| Type | Fresh / Experienced / Transfer | Pick one |
| Status | Available / Reserved / Placed | Only "Available" appears on the website |
| Consent | "She agrees to her profile, photo and video being published" | **Required** – nothing is published without it |

## 2. Photo

- One clear, recent photo of her face and shoulders, **portrait (upright)**.
- Plain background, good lighting, her own photo only.
- JPG or PNG, under 2 MB.

## 3. Personal particulars

Height, weight, religion, marital status, number of children, education level.

## 4. Work experience

For each past job:

- Country (e.g. Singapore)
- Years (e.g. 2020 – 2024)
- Duties (e.g. "Cared for 2 children aged 3 and 6, cooking, housework")

For a fresh helper, write "No overseas experience".

## 5. Skills

Give each a level: *Experienced / Some experience / Basic / Willing to learn / No*.

- Childcare (note infant care if applicable)
- Eldercare (note bedridden / wheelchair care if applicable)
- Cooking (list cuisines)
- Pets
- Housekeeping

Also choose her **3 key skills** to show on her card.

## 6. Languages

e.g. Burmese (native), English (basic), Mandarin (a little).

## 7. Preferences

- Rest days per month
- Can handle dogs? Cats?
- Other notes (e.g. "can handle pork", "prefers a family with young children")

## 8. Availability

e.g. "Available immediately", "From 15 November", "4–6 weeks (fresh)".

## 9. Agency remarks

1–3 sentences in your words.

## 10. Introduction video (optional)

- 30–60 seconds, MP4, filmed **upright** on a phone; 720p is enough.
- **Under 20 MB** (GitHub refuses files over 100 MB, and big videos load slowly).
- She introduces herself: first name, age, experience, skills – ideally in English, in a quiet, well-lit room.
- Must not show or mention: surname, passport, FIN, address, phone number, social media, or her
  current employer's name or home.
- Either attach the MP4, or upload it to YouTube as **Unlisted** and send the link.

## Never send

These will not be published even if sent, so it's best not to send them at all:

- Passport, FIN or work permit numbers
- Exact date of birth
- Home or employer address
- Her phone number, email or social media
- Medical records or health details

## Example message

> **New helper: KD-1825**
> Thandar, 30, Myanmar, Experienced, Available. Consent: yes.
> Height 155 cm, 50 kg, Buddhist, single, no children, high school.
> Experience: Singapore 2020–2024, cared for 2 children, cooking and housework.
> Skills: childcare – experienced; eldercare – willing to learn; cooking – experienced (Chinese, Burmese);
> pets – some experience; housekeeping – experienced. Key skills: childcare, cooking, housekeeping.
> Languages: Burmese (native), English (basic).
> Rest days: 2/month. Dogs: yes. Cats: yes. Can handle pork.
> Available immediately.
> Remarks: Cheerful and patient, good references.
> *(photo and video attached)*

## Quick updates

- "KD-1825 is now reserved" – hides her from the listing
- "KD-1825 is placed" – hides her from the listing
- "KD-1825 is available again" – shows her again
- "Change KD-1825's availability to 1 December"
