About us

Care that feels like kin

Kin Domestic Pte. Ltd. is a licensed Singapore employment agency (EA Licence No. 26C3706) that matches families with caring, reliable Myanmar domestic helpers.

Our name says what we believe: the person who helps care for your home, your children and your parents should feel like part of the family, like kin.

We keep things simple for employers. Our agency fees are upfront, with no hidden charges. We bring your helper to your door at no extra cost, and if things don't work out we offer a free replacement within 12 months.
