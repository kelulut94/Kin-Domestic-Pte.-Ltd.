FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between fresh, experienced and transfer helpers?

Fresh helpers are working overseas for the first time. Experienced helpers have worked overseas before. Transfer helpers are already working in Singapore and are looking for new employers.

What does the $588 agency fee package include?

It covers our agency service for a fresh or experienced Myanmar helper, including free doorstep handover and a free replacement within 12 months. Government and third-party costs such as the work permit, medical and insurance are listed separately on our fees page.

What is the placement fee?

For fresh and experienced helpers, the placement fee is an advance paid on the helper's behalf and recovered through her monthly salary over 2–6 months, so it is not ultimately borne by the employer. Transfer helpers have no placement fee.

How do I view helper biodata?

Browse our helper biodata online and filter by type, nationality and skills. Biodata is shared with the helper's consent; contact us for full details.

How does the free replacement work?

If the match isn't right, we provide a free replacement within 12 months. WhatsApp us for the full terms.
